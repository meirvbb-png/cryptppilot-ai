# ToolScout editorial and monetization plan

## Niche
Practical software and productivity tools for freelancers and micro-businesses, supported by free browser calculators. English and Hebrew editions.

## Monetization — not active yet
- Apply to legitimate software affiliate programs only after publishing useful original content.
- Never fabricate affiliate URLs, prices, features, reviews or commission rates.
- Clearly disclose affiliate relationships and use rel="sponsored nofollow".
- Verify provider terms and applicable disclosure requirements.
- Keep editorial rankings independent of commission rates.

## Editorial backlog
1. Best free invoicing tools for freelancers — Israel and international editions.
2. Free project-management apps — limits, exports and collaboration restrictions.
3. Free CRM tools for a one-person business.
4. Appointment scheduling tools — free-plan caps and integrations.
5. AI meeting-note tools — language support, privacy and data retention.
6. Email marketing platforms — list-size and sending limits.

## Comparison protocol
Record the date checked, official pricing URL, tested workflows, free-plan limits, language support, export options, privacy/security documentation, cancellation terms, support and limitations. Do not publish a ranking until hands-on checks are complete.

## Disclosure template
Some links may be affiliate links. If you purchase through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This does not determine our ratings. We only recommend products assessed against the criteria described on this page.
